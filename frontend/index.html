NEXUS BI

AI-Powered Business Intelligence Platform

Sales, customers, logistics and machine-learning models for Olist, a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace. Every figure on these pages is computed from the data stored in PostgreSQL.

What you can answer

  • Ask your dataQuestions in plain language, answered with SQL you can inspect and figures checked against the results.
  • OverviewSeven KPIs against the previous period and the year before, with findings computed for the period.
  • SalesRevenue, orders, ticket size, categories and the weekly buying rhythm.
  • CustomersNew and returning buyers, lifetime value and cohort retention.
  • ProductsBest sellers, most profitable, and products that stopped selling.
  • GeographyAn interactive map of sales, growth and delivery performance.
  • SegmentsRFM segments with thresholds taken from the real repurchase cycle.
  • Churn riskWho is unlikely to buy again, and why, with honest model metrics.
  • ForecastEight weeks of daily revenue, with a range taken from past errors.
  • AnomaliesDays, products and customers that broke their own pattern.
  • Data qualityEvery problem found in the raw files, and what was done about it.

How the numbers are made

  1. Raw filesNine Olist CSV files
  2. Validation and cleaningEvery fix is logged
  3. PostgreSQLNormalised model and SQL views
  4. ModelsSegments, churn, forecast, anomalies
  5. APIRead-only FastAPI
  6. DashboardWhat you are looking at

Data: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Profit and margin are estimates from a synthetic cost model, because Olist does not publish product costs; every other metric is real.
